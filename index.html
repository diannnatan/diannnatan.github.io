About Me

I am a translational research scientist trained in cell and gene therapy. My academic training at University College London focused on recombinant adeno-associated virus (rAAV)-based gene therapies, building a strong foundation in vector development and advanced therapy manufacturing. This experience shaped my interest in in vivo gene therapies as a promising platform for next-generation genetic medicines.

Currently, I serve as Associate Senior Scientist and Study Director at WuXi AppTec's In Vivo Pharmacology Unit, supporting preclinical programs in oncology and immunology. I design and oversee in vivo efficacy and PK/PD studies across antibody-drug conjugates and cell and gene therapy platforms, contributing to data-driven progression toward clinical development.

In the long term, I aim to advance clinically translatable genetic therapies by integrating molecular engineering, delivery optimization, and rigorous preclinical evaluation—bridging scientific innovation with therapeutic impact.
