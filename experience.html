📚 Education
MSc Manufacture and Commercialization of Cell and Gene Therapies
University College London (UCL) 📍 London, UK

During my MSc, I was exposed to a broad range of advanced therapy modalities and became particularly drawn to in vivo gene delivery. Compared with ex vivo or cell-based approaches, in vivo strategies offer systemic accessibility beyond hematopoietic cells, lower risks associated with genomic integration, tunable gene expression, and the potential for scalable, standardized manufacturing. These advantages reflect not only biological precision but also sound engineering principles — qualities that align closely with my long-term scientific interests.

Motivated by this direction, I focused my master's project on recombinant adeno-associated virus (rAAV) upstream process optimization, working directly with the leading vector platform used in clinically approved in vivo therapies. This experience further solidified my commitment to advancing scalable and clinically translatable genetic medicines.

📚 Education
BSc Biological Sciences: Cell Biology
University College London (UCL) 📍 London, UK

During my undergraduate studies, I began attending seminars and symposia across London, where I encountered CAR-T cell therapy. One memory remains vivid: at the 2022 King's College Hospital Advanced Therapies Symposium, a patient who had undergone CAR-T cell therapy shared how the treatment had given him a second chance at life. Seeing him alive, healthy, and grateful was the moment that turned my curiosity into conviction, and it became the turning point that focused my path on translational science.

From then on, I made a sustained effort to ground this fascination into expertise. I took every course I could find that touched on immunology and cancer therapeutics, and for my undergraduate thesis, I deliberately stepped outside the typical departmental offerings to pursue a project in the Department of Biochemical Engineering, examining CAR-T cell manufacturing. That experience opened my eyes to the translational challenges of advanced therapies and motivated me to pursue a master's degree dedicated to CGT.
